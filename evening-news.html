Evening News

Evening News is a collaboration between disquette•s and JD Bolland, built around a late-night atmosphere and a beat inspired by the jazz-funk and city pop sounds of the mid-1980s.

Sleek synths, a driving groove, lush organic pop vocals and layered harmonies are filtered through a modern house music sensibility. Repetition and hypnotic patterns give the track a cinematic, nocturnal character.

artists

disquette•s

disquette•s is the moniker of Italian producer and DJ Luigi Porcino. His work draws from a wide range of electronic and dance music, with a particular fascination for obscure sounds from the 70s, 80s and 90s.

His productions move between synth-pop, Italo disco, chillwave, house and left-field electronic music, combining vintage influences with a contemporary production approach.

JD Bolland

JD Bolland is a songwriter, vocalist and producer. His work is characterised by a distinctive, organic vocal sound, falsetto and densely layered harmonies.

Idiosyncratic lyrics and interweaving vocal arrangements are recurring elements of his songwriting.

Together, these elements give his work an instantly recognisable identity across different musical settings.
